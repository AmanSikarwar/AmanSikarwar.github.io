Invoice reimbursement system that analyzes PDF invoices against HR policies
with Gemini: batch ZIP uploads with duplicate detection, sentence-transformer
embeddings stored in Qdrant, a RAG-powered chatbot for querying processed
invoices in natural language, streaming responses end to end, and a
Streamlit frontend over a documented FastAPI backend — fully Dockerized.
