Pure SwiftUI app over the Jikan API: debounced real-time search, category
browsing with infinite scroll and pull-to-refresh, detail pages with
characters and recommendations, Core Data favorites, memory/disk image
caching, and client-side API rate limiting — MVVM throughout.
