Deep-learning research on translating EEG brain activity into natural
language and classifying sentiment straight from brain signals: BART- and
T5-based BrainTranslator seq2seq decoders trained on the ZuCo dataset,
MLP/LSTM/BERT sentiment baselines, and a zero-shot pipeline that decodes EEG
to text before classifying it — evaluated with BLEU, ROUGE and WER.
