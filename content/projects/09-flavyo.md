Cross-platform app connecting distributors, vendors, and sales executives in
one live workflow: role-based catalogs and pricing, real-time inventory and
order sync via Supabase Realtime, idempotent checkout, full order lifecycle
from pending to delivered, and a distributor KPI dashboard — clean
architecture with row-level security throughout.
